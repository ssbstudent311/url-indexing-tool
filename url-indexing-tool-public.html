● FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I submit a backlink from another site?

Yes — any public URL, no Search Console needed. Only IndexNow and Bing Submit need your own site. Indexing is still Google’s decision.

How does the Hub Page Engine work?

It builds a page on your site with a dofollow link to the backlink. Download it, upload it to /hub/ on your site, then run “Hub live check” — only then do Tier-2 signals fire.

What are crawl pages?

The method real backlink indexers use: your submitted links are published on crawl pages you control — up to 50 links per page, each with a title, a short description and a dofollow link — and the crawl pages themselves are submitted to search engines (and listed in the hub feed.xml), so crawlers discover every link on them. Build them in the Crawl Pages section, upload the files to your site, then use “Check live + submit”. A crawl page is a discovery pathway — it cannot guarantee that Google indexes the links.

What are Smart mode and the sitemap tools?

Smart mode checks first and submits only unindexed URLs. Sitemap import auto-discovers sitemaps from robots.txt, checks sitemap health (200 + valid XML), and keeps only new or changed URLs by lastmod — only those go to IndexNow. A saved sitemap auto-queues new URLs only, and a dedupe cache skips anything already submitted today or already indexed.

What signals are sent?

Archive.org, WebSub, Twingly, Ping-o-matic, IndexNow (own site — sent to both bing.com/indexnow and the central api.indexnow.org, which distributes to Yandex, Seznam.cz and Naver), Bing SubmitUrlBatch (own site, backend key), Mass Ping across 18 endpoints, and the Google Indexing API when you load your own service-account key in the browser panel. Each reports its own status in Tracking.

Does Guaranteed mean 100% indexing? What are credits?

No — no tool can force Google to index, and Guaranteed does not promise forced indexing. The guarantee is a refund promise: submit in Guaranteed mode and if the URL is not indexed within 24 hours, its held credits are refunded automatically and its row is marked Refunded — while free retries keep running every 6 hours to day 7 at no extra charge (if it indexes later, it costs nothing extra). Still-unindexed URLs also auto re-queue at day 10, 20, 30, 45 and 60. A URL that fails the pre-submit indexability filter (not a live 200, noindex, blocked by robots.txt, redirect loop) is rejected before any signal is sent and refunded instantly. Google API submissions use your own service-account keys, loaded and stored only in your browser (up to 10 projects, 200/day each — one Google account can hold up to 12 projects, up to 2,400/day; quota meters in Tracking), and a 200 from Google means accepted for crawling only. Credits are reference only (Normal 1 · Instant 3 · Guaranteed 7), held on submit and charged only on confirmed evidence. No payment here.

What does “Crawled” mean, and how long does indexing take?

Crawled means an archive copy was found — a crawler fetched the page. Google indexing is verified via each row’s site: check. Timing is Google’s: hours to days, sometimes never for thin or blocked pages.
