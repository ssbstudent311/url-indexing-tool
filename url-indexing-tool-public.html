● FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I submit a backlink from another site?

Yes — any public URL, no Search Console needed. Only IndexNow and Bing Submit need your own site. Indexing is still Google’s decision.

How does the Hub Page Engine work?

It builds a page on your site with a dofollow link to the backlink. Download it, upload it to /hub/ on your site, then run “Hub live check” — only then do Tier-2 signals fire.

What are Smart mode and the sitemap tools?

Smart mode checks first and submits only unindexed URLs. Sitemap import auto-discovers sitemaps from robots.txt, checks sitemap health (200 + valid XML), and keeps only new or changed URLs by lastmod — only those go to IndexNow. A saved sitemap auto-queues new URLs only, and a dedupe cache skips anything already submitted today or already indexed.

What signals are sent?

Archive.org, WebSub, Twingly, Ping-o-matic, IndexNow (own site), Bing SubmitUrlBatch (own site, backend key) and Mass Ping across 18 endpoints. Each reports its own status in Tracking.

Does Guaranteed mean 100% indexing? What are credits?

No — Guaranteed means retries every 6 hours for 7 days; still unindexed URLs also auto re-queue at day 10, 20, 30, 45 and 60. Google API submissions rotate across multiple backend keys (200/day each — quota meters in Tracking). Credits are reference only (Normal 1 · Instant 3 · Guaranteed 7), held on submit, charged on evidence, refunded at day 7 if none. No payment here.

What does “Crawled” mean, and how long does indexing take?

Crawled means an archive copy was found — a crawler fetched the page. Google indexing is verified via each row’s site: check. Timing is Google’s: hours to days, sometimes never for thin or blocked pages.
