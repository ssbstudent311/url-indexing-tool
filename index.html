Har URL ko Googlebot tak pohchao (URL & Backlink Indexer — kisi bhi domain ke liye)

Backlink ho ya apni site ka page — URL paste karo, mode chuno, aur tool 7 strong discovery signals ek sath bhejta hai: Archive.org, archive.today, Ping-o-matic, WebSub, Twingly, Blo.gs aur IndexNow. Backlink ho to Hub Page Engine us ke liye tumhari apni site par hub page banata hai — asal title, description aur dofollow link ke sath — taake Googlebot hub se backlink wale page tak pohche. Status par jhoota “Indexed” kabhi nahi likha jata: evidence mile to sirf “Crawled — Google confirm nahi”.

Hub Page Engine — Backlink Indexing ka Tagra Setup Random / backlink URLs ke liye

Random link (kisi aur ki site ka backlink) Google API se direct index nahi hota — us ka asal tareeqa hub page hai, aur ye engine wahi kaam auto karta hai. Har submitted URL ke liye tool target page ka asal title aur description fetch karke ek mukammal hub page banata hai: us backlink ka dofollow link, hub page ke apne meta tags, canonical aur robots index,follow ke sath. Phir hub page ko tumhari apni indexed site se Google tak push kiya jata hai — Googlebot hub page crawl karke usi dofollow link se backlink wale page tak pohchta hai.

Yehi address har hub page ka canonical banega aur RSS feed isi par base hogi. Base na likho to files ban jayengi lekin canonical me placeholder https://apki-site.com/hub/ aayega — upload se pehle asal address likh kar hub dobara bana lena.

Step 1Backlink URL submit karo — tool khud hub page banayega (asal title + description ke sath).
Step 2Hub page ki .html file download karo (har row me button hai, ya neeche se saari ek sath).
Step 3File ko apni site ke /hub/ folder me upload karo — ye step lazmi hai, is ke baghair push nahi hoga.
Step 4Yahan “Hub live check” dabao — file live ho to Tier-2 signals (archive.today hub copy, Archive.org, WebSub, Ping-o-matic, Twingly, Blo.gs) hub URL par parallel jayenge.
Step 5Googlebot hub tak pohchta hai — Tier-2 signals aur hub RSS feed crawlers ko hub page ki khabar dete hain; Googlebot hub crawl karke dofollow link se backlink tak jata hai. Guaranteed mode me hub live ho to yehi push har 6 ghante dobara hota hai, 7 din tak.

RSS feed ka naam feed.xml hai — isay bhi apni site ke /hub/ folder me upload karna hai, taake is ka address hub/feed.xml bane (masalan https://apnisite.com/hub/feed.xml). Har naya hub page is feed me item ban kar shamil hota hai — crawlers aur WebSub isi feed se naye hub pages uthate hain. Tracking ke liye neeche queue ki har row me hub ka status live dikhta hai: hub bana ya nahi · live hai ya nahi · Tier-2 signals gaye ya nahi.

Saaf baat: Hub file sirf tumhare computer me download hoti hai — jab tak tum usay apni site ke /hub/ folder me upload nahi karte, wo internet par live nahi hoti aur koi push kaam nahi karega. Upload ke baad hi live check aur Tier-2 signals asal maano me chalenge. Aur index ka final faisla phir bhi Google ka hai — hub page us tak pohchne ka sab se mazboot rasta hai, guarantee nahi.

● Teen Modes

Raftaar tumhari marzi ki

Paid indexer wale hi teen modes — lekin yahan koi credit, koi payment nahi. Har mode me wahi 7 strong signals jate hain.

N

Normal — ~5–10 ghante

Signals ek ke baad ek, waqfay ke sath. Services par bojh kam, aur aam tor par Googlebot kuch ghanton me page utha leta hai. Roz ke routine backlinks ke liye behtareen.

I

Instant — minutes

Submit dabate hi Archive.org, archive.today, Ping-o-matic, WebSub, Twingly, Blo.gs — sab ek sath parallel. Discovery sab se jaldi shuru hoti hai; crawl phir bhi Googlebot ke schedule par hai.

G

Guaranteed — 7 din, har 6 ghante retry

Backlinks ke liye sab se tagra mode. Instant blast ke baad tool 7 din tak har 6 ghante har backlink ko auto re-check karta hai. Archive copy (crawl evidence) na mile to Archive.org, archive.today hub copy, WebSub, Ping-o-matic, Twingly aur Blo.gs — saare signals backlink par dobara fire hote hain, aur hub page live ho to hub par bhi dobara push hota hai. Retry tab tak chalta hai jab tak evidence na mile ya 7 din poore na ho jayen. Har row me Guaranteed deadline, agla retry aur kitni dafa retry hua live dikhta hai. Page khula rakhna parega — band kiya to watch wahin ruk jayegi. Guaranteed ka matlab 7 din tak musalsal retry hai, 100% index ka wada nahi — final faisla Google ka hai.

● Fast URL Discovery

Teen steps — paste, signal, check

Google kisi page ko tab index karta hai jab pehle usay crawl kare. Crawl tab hota hai jab Googlebot ko URL ka pata chale. Ye tool wahi “pata chalana” wala kaam karta hai — 7 alag raston se, ek sath.

1

URLs add karo

Ek URL ya poori list paste karo — backlinks, naye pages, CSV/TXT file ya sitemap import. Duplicates aur galat URLs tool khud alag kar deta hai.

2

7 signals jate hain

Do archive services page ki public copy banati hain, chaar ping services update ki khabar har taraf phailati hain, aur apni site ho to IndexNow search engines ko foran batata hai.

3

Status live check karo

Archive copy mil jaye to status “Crawled — Google confirm nahi” banta hai. Tool kabhi jhoota “Indexed” nahi likhta — Google index ki tasdeeq sirf “Google check” (site:) link se hoti hai.

Archive.org Save Signal 1

Internet Archive ko page fetch karke save karne ki request jati hai. Public copy ban jana hi sab se mazboot crawl evidence hai — status “Crawled” isi copy ke milne par banta hai, andazay par nahi.

archive.today Hub Copy Signal 2

Doosra bara archive network — page ki hub copy submit hoti hai. Do alag archive crawlers ka page fetch karna discovery ko dugna mazboot karta hai, aur copy baad me khud khol kar dekhi ja sakti hai.

Ping-o-matic Signal 3

Update-service ping jo naye content ki khabar kai services tak pohchata hai. Browser is ka jawab parh nahi sakta, is liye log me ye hamesha “bheja gaya (unverified)” likha jata hai.

WebSub (PubSubHubbub) Signal 4

Google ke apne hub ko publish ping jata hai — feed/update subscribers ko URL foran mil jata hai. Ye wahi protocol hai jo bade publishers naye content ke liye use karte hain.

Twingly Ping Signal 5

Blog / content search network ko XML-RPC ping — URL unke crawl system me chala jata hai, jahan se doosre crawlers bhi uthate hain.

Blo.gs Ping Signal 6

Sab se purani update ping service — weblog update ki khabar unke directory network tak. Chhota signal hai, lekin har extra rasta discovery ke chances barhata hai.

IndexNow Signal 7 · Apni site

Apni site ke liye sab se seedha rasta: IndexNow Bing, Yandex waghera ko foran URL bata deta hai. Key file apni site par hona zaroori hai — tool key file aur payload dono bana deta hai, key sirf is session me rehti hai.

Hub Page + RSS Feed Backlink · Tier-2

Random backlink ke liye tool tumhari site ke liye hub page banata hai — target ka asal title/description, dofollow link, canonical, robots index,follow. File /hub/ folder me upload karne ke baad hub URL par wahi 6 Tier-2 signals (archive.today hub copy, Archive.org, WebSub, Ping-o-matic, Twingly, Blo.gs) parallel jate hain, aur har hub page hub/feed.xml RSS feed me shamil hota hai.

Seedhi baat — “Indexed” ka jhoota dawa nahi

Ye signals discovery aur crawl karwate hain — index karna ya na karna hamesha Google ka apna faisla hai, jo page ka content, quality aur site ki authority dekh kar hota hai. Is liye tool ke status sirf teen hain: Pending (signals gaye, crawl ka intezar), Crawled — Google confirm nahi (archive copy mil gayi, matlab crawler page fetch kar chuka hai) aur Not indexed (check par koi evidence nahi mila). Guaranteed mode ke liye bhi yahi sach hai: Guaranteed ka matlab hai ke tool 7 din tak har 6 ghante signals aur hub push dobara fire karta rahega jab tak evidence na mile — ye 100% index ka wada nahi hai, aur final index decision hamesha Google ki hai. Jo tool “2–5 minute me 100% index guarantee” ka dawa kare, wo dawa verify nahi hota — yahan har signal ka asal natija screen par dikhta hai.

● Hub Page Engine — Backlinks Ke Liye

Backlink index karwana hai? Rasta hub page hai

Backlink kisi aur ki site par hota hai — wo tumhari apni site nahi hoti, is liye usay direct Google ko submit nahi kar sakte. RankLocal / MonkeyIndexer jesi services bhi asal me yahi karti hain: backlink ko apni kisi indexed site ke page (hub) se jod kar us hub ko Google tak push karti hain. Ye engine wahi setup tumhari apni site par banata hai.

1

Hub page auto banta hai

Backlink submit karte hi tool target page ko fetch karke us ka asal title aur description nikaalta hai aur ek mukammal hub page banata hai — dofollow link, meta description, canonical aur robots index,follow ke sath. Fetch na ho sake (site browser ko rok de) to URL se bana hua title use hota hai aur row me saaf likha aata hai ke asal title nahi mila.

2

/hub/ folder me upload — lazmi step

Hub page ki .html file download karke apni site ke /hub/ folder me upload karo (WordPress ho to hosting ke File Manager / FTP se public_html/hub/). Sath me feed.xml bhi usi folder me rakho. Upload se pehle hub page internet par exist hi nahi karta — push us ke baad hota hai.

3

Push: signals + RSS

File live ho jaye to queue ki row se “Hub live check” dabao — Tier-2 signals (archive.today hub copy, Archive.org, WebSub, Ping-o-matic, Twingly, Blo.gs) hub URL par parallel jate hain, aur hub/feed.xml RSS feed crawlers ko har naye hub page ki khabar deti rehti hai. Har backlink ka hub status — bana / live / signals gaye — usi ki row me track hota hai.

Hub page kyun kaam karta hai

Googlebot un pages ko pehle crawl karta hai jo kisi pehle se indexed, trusted site par hote hain. Tumhari site ka hub page jab signals aur RSS feed se Google tak pohchta hai, to Googlebot wahan se dofollow link follow karke backlink wale page tak jata hai — yahi discovery backlink ko index ke qabil banati hai. Isi liye hub wali site jitni purani aur indexed hogi, backlink utni jaldi utha jata hai. Final faisla phir bhi Google ka hai — backlink wale page ka content aur quality bhi matter karti hai.

● Sawal Jawab

Aksar pooche jane wale sawal

Kya doosri site ka backlink bhi submit ho sakta hai?

Haan — kisi bhi public URL ko GSC ke baghair submit kar sakte ho, kyunki archive aur ping signals ko ownership proof nahi chahiye. Sirf IndexNow apni site ke liye hai (key file chahiye hoti hai). Index ka faisla phir bhi Google karega, aur jis page par backlink hai us ki quality matter karti hai.

Hub Page Engine kya hai aur backlink ke liye kyun zaroori hai?

Random / backlink URL kisi aur ki site par hota hai — wo tumhari apni site nahi hoti, is liye usay direct Google ko submit nahi kar sakte. Hub Page Engine us backlink ke liye tumhari apni site par ek page banata hai jisme backlink ka dofollow link hota hai. Us hub page ko signals aur RSS se Googlebot tak pohchaya jata hai, aur Googlebot hub se backlink tak pohchta hai. Paid indexer services bhi asal me yahi karti hain — farq ye hai ke wahan hub unki site par banta hai, yahan tumhari apni site par.

Hub file upload kiye baghair push ho jayega?

Nahi — aur ye sab se aam galti hai. Tool hub page ki .html file tumhare browser me banata hai; jab tak tum us file ko apni site ke /hub/ folder me upload nahi karte, wo page internet par live hi nahi hota. Pehle upload karo, phir yahan “Hub live check” se confirm karo, phir Tier-2 signals chalao — push tabhi asal hoga. RSS feed (feed.xml) bhi usi /hub/ folder me upload karni hai.

Hub page me target ka asal title/description kaise aata hai?

Hub banate waqt tool target page ko browser se fetch karke us ka <title> aur meta description parhta hai aur wahi hub page ke title, heading aur description me lagata hai. Kuch sites browser fetch ko rok deti hain (CORS) — tab tool public proxy se try karta hai, aur phir bhi na mile to URL se bana hua title use hota hai; queue ki row me saaf likha hota hai ke asal title mila ya fallback use hua.

Normal, Instant aur Guaranteed me asal farq kya hai?

Signals teeno me wahi 7 hain. Normal me wo ek ke baad ek waqfay se jate hain (~5–10 ghante me crawl aam hai). Instant me submit karte hi sab parallel chal jate hain. Guaranteed — backlinks ke liye sab se tagra — me Instant ke baad 7 din tak har 6 ghante auto re-check chalta hai: evidence na mile to backlink par saare signals dobara fire hote hain aur hub page live ho to hub par bhi dobara push hota hai, jab tak evidence na mile ya deadline poori na ho. Har row me Guaranteed deadline (7 din), agla retry kab hai aur kitni dafa retry hua — sab live dikhta hai.

Kya Guaranteed ka matlab 100% index hai?

Nahi — saaf baat: final index decision Google ki hai, koi tool ya service 100% index ka sach-much wada nahi kar sakti. Guaranteed ka matlab yahan sirf ye hai ke tool 7 din tak har 6 ghante musalsal retry karta rahega — signals dobara fire, hub page live ho to dobara push, aur har retry ka count tracking me dikhega — jab tak crawl evidence na mile ya 7 din poore na ho jayen.

“Crawled — Google confirm nahi” ka matlab kya hai?

Matlab archive service ne page fetch karke us ki copy bana li hai — kisi crawler ne page parh liya hai, ye sabit hai. Lekin Google ke index me page aaya ya nahi, ye browser tool confirm nahi kar sakta. Har URL ke “Google check” link se site: search karke khud tasdeeq karo.

Guaranteed mode ke liye page khula rakhna kyun parega?

Ye public static tool hai — koi server peeche nahi chal raha jo tumhare liye check karta rahe. Auto re-check aur har 6 ghante wala retry isi khuli hui tab me chalta hai. Page band kar diya to watch aur retry wahin ruk jayenge; dobara khol kar URL phir Guaranteed me submit karna parega. Isi liye “Guaranteed” ki guarantee 7 din tak musalsal retry ki hai, index ki nahi — final faisla Google ka hai.

Kitni dair me index hoga?

Koi fix time nahi. Crawl kabhi minutes-ghanton me, kabhi dinon me hota hai, aur kuch URLs index hi nahi hote (patla content, noindex, robots block). Instant mode discovery foran shuru kar deta hai — us ke baad waqt Googlebot ka hai.

Page 404 / noindex ho to kya hoga?

Signals chale bhi jayen to Google aise page ko index nahi karega. Pehle ye check karo: page live hai (200 status), meta robots me noindex nahi, aur robots.txt crawl block nahi kar raha.

Ye data kahan save hota hai?

Kahin nahi. Queue, status aur IndexNow key sirf is khuli hui tab me rehte hain, page band karte hi khatam. Apna record rakhna ho to “Report CSV” download kar lo — us me har URL ka mode, status aur har signal ka natija hota hai.

Ek batch me kitne URLs bhej sakta hoon?

List me 200 tak URLs add ho jate hain; signals ek run me pehle 50 par jate hain (services par bojh na pare). Baqi queue me rehte hain — dobara button dabane par agle 50 chalte hain.
